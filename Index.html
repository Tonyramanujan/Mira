Chapter One

How It All Started ❤️

👽 An Alien Meets His Earthling

Who knew that a simple meeting on Bottled would turn into something this beautiful? We started as two strangers, exchanged Instagram, and slowly started knowing each other. I never imagined that a random meeting could turn into so many little memories.

📞 Seven Little Calls

Almost seven calls, countless little conversations, teasing, smiling and those moments when neither of us wanted the conversation to end. Somehow, talking to you started feeling special. Sometimes I wish those calls had a pause button.

🌍 Alien & Earthling

You became my Earthling, and I became your Alien. It's such a silly little name, but now it carries some of my favourite memories. Maybe we belong to different worlds, but I like the little universe we created through our conversations.

😊 That Blushing Moment

You teasing me, noticing my shyness, and knowing when I blush... I don't know how you do that, but you make even my silent reactions special. And yes, I know you enjoy teasing your Alien! 😄

📱 Five Days of Missing You

When my phone stopped working, our conversations suddenly paused. I didn't expect those little conversations to be missed this much. And knowing that you missed me too made me smile in a way I can't explain. I'm glad we're talking again. ❤️

Something From My Heart

"I didn't plan to make you a part of my days. It just happened... Somewhere between our conversations, your teasing, your smile and your little moments, you became someone I genuinely look forward to. And honestly, I'm happy that our paths crossed."

My 2–3 AM Thoughts

The Moon Reminds Me of You

There were a few nights when I couldn't sleep. Around 2 or 3 AM, I would look at the moon. And somehow, the quiet moonlit sky reminded me of you. I don't know why... Maybe because even when you are not around, some thoughts of you find their way into my nights. Sometimes I wished you were there, just to share that little silence with me. No big conversations, no special plans... Just you, me and that beautiful moon.

Maybe that's one of the little things I wanted to tell you. 🌙❤️

One Thing I Want You To Know

I know there are days when work gets tiring, when things feel heavy, and when you don't feel like yourself. You don't always have to be cheerful around me. You can talk, complain, stay quiet, or simply be yourself. You don't have to hide your low days or pretend everything is okay.

I may not have a solution to everything, but I would love to listen and be there for you. I don't want you to feel that you have to face every difficult moment alone.

I can't promise that every day will be perfect, but I can promise to be honest with you, to listen, to understand and to make an effort. And I hope we keep choosing to understand each other, even on the not-so-perfect days. ❤️
