The Story of Vaasu

Every birth has a story. Some begin with a dream, some with a prayer, and some with the blessings of God. This is the story of a mother's faith, her beautiful journey, and a little boy named Vaasu.

Before the story begins...

Three little Krishnas are waiting for you. Choose any one to open the story.

🦚 May Krishna's blessings guide this little journey.

CHAPTER 01 · THE BEGINNING
🌾🐄

A Little Girl From A Beautiful Village

Do you know what happened on this day, many years ago? Once upon a time, there lived a very innocent girl in a beautiful village surrounded by greenery. She knew very little about the materialistic and luxurious world. Her little universe was her family, her sisters and brothers, the green agricultural fields, and the cows that filled her days with happiness. She found joy in the simplest things. Her childhood was filled with nature, innocence and the warmth of family. She didn't know what the future had planned for her.

🦚
CHAPTER 02 · A NEW JOURNEY
🌸💍

A New Chapter In Her Life

As time passed, she turned fifteen. At such a young age, her life took a completely new direction. She married a young, handsome man, an intelligent person who had built his life through his own hard work. The first few days after their marriage were beautiful. Soon, he had to leave for work, while she stayed back at home. A year later, he returned for her, and together they travelled to Mumbai for some time. Eventually, their journey brought them back to Hyderabad. A new place, a new life, and many dreams still waiting to unfold.

🪈
CHAPTER 03 · A MOTHER'S HEART
🙏🦚

Her Faith Never Faded

As the years passed, she longed to become a mother. Sometimes, the words of people around her hurt deeply. Her aunt would scold her about not having a child, and there were moments when she quietly cried behind closed doors. But she never stopped praying. Since childhood, she had been deeply devoted to Lord Krishna. No matter how difficult a day had been, she would make time for her daily pooja. Her circumstances changed, but her faith never did. Every day, she placed her hopes, her tears and her dreams at Krishna's feet. And she continued to believe.

"A prayer spoken in silence is never small in the eyes of faith."
CHAPTER 04 · A BEAUTIFUL BLESSING
👶❤️

Her First Little Miracle

And one day, her prayers were answered. At around twenty years of age, she was blessed with her first child. A beautiful little baby boy arrived in their lives. He was fair, cute and adorable. The happiness of becoming parents filled their hearts. They loved their little one with all the affection they had. Their home was now filled with a new kind of happiness. Sleepless nights, little cries, tiny smiles and endless love... A whole new world had begun.

🦚
CHAPTER 05 · A DIVINE THOUGHT
🦚🪈

A Mother's Love And Krishna's Grace

In the years that followed, the parents faced many sleepless nights while raising their little one. The mother sometimes became anxious and worried about her child. And in the quiet moments of her devotion, she continued to seek Krishna's blessings. In the beautiful way she imagined her faith, it was as though Lord Krishna looked upon his devotee and understood her heart. Perhaps he wanted to bless her once more... With a little soul who would grow up to love her deeply, care for her, and bring her happiness. And once again, life began to unfold a beautiful new chapter. She became pregnant.

"Some blessings arrive as answers to prayers we never stopped believing in."
CHAPTER 06 · NATURE'S WHISPER
🍃🌼

When Nature Was Waiting

The days passed. Karthika Masam had come to an end, and Mrigashira Masam was about to begin. A gentle winter breeze travelled through the surroundings. The world seemed to slow down. The trees stood quietly, the fields rested beneath the morning sky, and nature seemed to be waiting for something beautiful. When everything around lies in silence, new leaves begin to sprout from every tree. Every flower waits for the warmth of the sun, longing for the moment when its buds finally bloom. And somewhere, a little life was preparing to enter this world.

🦚
CHAPTER 07 · THE MAGICAL MORNING
🌑✨

November 27 · 4:00 AM

And then came that beautiful morning. It was the 27th of November. The clock was approaching four in the morning. The sky was wrapped in darkness, and the Sun and Moon stood together in the quietness of the new-moon phase. The world was still asleep. The cool breeze moved gently, while a new chapter was about to begin inside a mother's life. And then... A little baby boy was born. A new soul had arrived. A new smile had entered the family. A new story had begun. A little boy with a slightly dusky complexion, an adorable face, and two beautiful dimples.

"While the world slept, a mother's world became brighter."
CHAPTER 08 · LITTLE MISCHIEF
👦🌸

A Little Stubborn, A Lot Of Love

As he slowly grew up, his little personality began to shine. He liked doing things by himself. He could be a little stubborn sometimes, but he was equally lovely. Those tiny dimples, his innocent face, his little mischief and his own ways of expressing love... Slowly, he became a beautiful part of his family's everyday life. A little boy with his own dreams, thoughts and imagination. And his mother had a special name for him.

🦚
CHAPTER 09 · THE NAME
🪈🙏

And She Named Him...

VAASU

A little name, carrying a beautiful story. A mother's journey. A mother's faith. A mother's prayers. And a little boy who arrived on a quiet November morning. Perhaps he will never completely know how much his arrival meant to her. But this is the story behind his birthday. This is the story of Vaasu.
